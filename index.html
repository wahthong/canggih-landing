About Us

Piling & Foundation Specialists You Can Trust

Established in 2009, Usahawan Canggih Sdn. Bhd. (UCSB) is a Sabah-based civil engineering firm specialising in piling and foundation works. From our Tawau headquarters, we serve the whole of Sabah — delivering reliable, quality-driven foundation solutions for government infrastructure, commercial developments, and residential projects.

Our core expertise lies in driving RC square piles and spun piles, backed by rigorous quality assurance and on-time delivery. With a fleet of modern hydraulic piling rigs and an experienced team, we bring the strength and reliability that every structure demands from its foundation.

What We Do

Our Core Services

From piling and foundation works to building construction and civil engineering — we deliver comprehensive solutions across every project phase.

Piling & Foundation Works

Our core speciality — driving RC square piles and spun piles with precision and efficiency using modern hydraulic hammers.

  • RC Square Pile (150mm–400mm)
  • Spun Pile Installation
  • Pile Load Testing (PDA/Static)
  • Value Engineering & Cost Optimisation

Building Construction

Full-scope building construction services for government and private developments, from substructure to superstructure completion.

  • Government Buildings (JKR/CIDB)
  • Commercial & Residential
  • Institutional Facilities
  • Design & Build Projects

Civil & Infrastructure Works

Infrastructure development including earthworks, drainage, roadworks, and site preparation for large-scale developments.

  • Earthworks & Site Preparation
  • Drainage & Roadworks
  • Soil Investigation (SI)
  • Geotechnical Engineering
