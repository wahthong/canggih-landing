Piling quotation FAQs

What do I need for a piling quotation?

Send the project location, pile drawings and BQ, specified pile sizes and quantities, target start date and site access details. Include the soil investigation report and testing requirements if available. Tell us if quantities are provisional.

Does Usahawan Canggih work outside Tawau?

We are based in Tawau and accept project enquiries across Sabah. Mobilisation, plant availability, access and programme are reviewed for each project; the site location does not represent a separate company branch.

What should a piling quotation include?

Check the stated scope for mobilisation, pile supply and delivery, handling, driving, joints, testing, records, setting out, as-built drawings and pile-head cutting. Inclusions, exclusions and rate-only items should be confirmed in the quotation.

Can I send drawings or a BQ through the website?

Use the enquiry form to prepare a WhatsApp message, then attach your drawings or BQ and press Send in WhatsApp. The website does not upload files or send the message automatically. You can also email wttee@canggihgroup.com.

Is a WhatsApp enquiry a confirmed booking?

No. An enquiry starts a discussion. Scope, pricing, programme and commercial terms must be agreed separately before work is confirmed.
