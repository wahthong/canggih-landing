Piling & foundation contractor in Sabah

Usahawan Canggih Sdn. Bhd. is a Tawau-based piling and foundation contractor serving projects across Sabah. We welcome enquiries from main contractors, developers, consultants and project owners for RC square pile and spun pile works.

A quotation built around your project

Send the piling drawings and bill of quantities, together with the project location and expected programme. These let us review the specified pile system, quantities, access and mobilisation requirements before preparing a project-specific quotation.

Our published piling services include RC square piles from 150 mm to 400 mm, spun pile installation and pile load testing. The available scope, plant arrangement and programme are confirmed for each enquiry.

What the piling scope can cover

  • Mobilisation and demobilisation of piling plant.
  • Supply and delivery of the specified precast concrete piles.
  • Pile driving, extensions and welded joints as specified.
  • Piling records and the agreed testing requirements.

Site preparation, working platforms, setting out, testing, pile-head cutting and as-built requirements should be identified separately in the enquiry. The quotation will state what is included, excluded or priced as a rate-only item.

Information to send for pricing

  1. Site location, access information and target start date.
  2. Pile layout, sections, specifications and BQ, if available.
  3. Soil investigation report and the consultant’s testing requirements, if available.
  4. Any site restrictions or main-contractor requirements.

If the design or quantities are not yet final, tell us what information is available so we can identify the missing pricing inputs. Pile selection and design acceptance remain subject to the project’s engineering requirements.
