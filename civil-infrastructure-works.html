Civil & infrastructure works in Sabah

Usahawan Canggih’s civil and infrastructure services cover earthworks, site preparation, drainage and roadworks. We welcome project enquiries alongside our piling and foundation work.

Define the work boundaries

Send the civil drawings, specifications and BQ with the site location. Identify whether the enquiry is for an individual work package or a combined civil and piling scope.

Our published service scope includes earthworks and site preparation, drainage and roadworks, as well as soil investigation and geotechnical enquiries. The particular work, resources and specialist arrangements are reviewed for each project.

Information that helps us prepare a quotation

  • Existing and proposed levels, work limits and quantities.
  • Material and construction specifications.
  • Access, disposal or delivery restrictions.
  • Required work sequence and target programme.
  • Testing, survey and handover documentation requirements.

Where the package interfaces with piling, clarify responsibility for working platforms, setting out and reinstatement. These should be agreed expressly rather than assumed to be part of mobilisation.
